The AI Fluency Framework for Healthcare Professionals

AI fluency means working with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically and safely. The framework breaks that into four everyday habits, known as the 4 Ds. This page walks through each one with clinical examples you can try, whatever your speciality.

About 10 minutes. Works offline. Tap a D to jump in.

Three ways of working with AI

The framework describes three modes of interaction. Knowing which one you are in tells you how much oversight is needed.

AI does a defined task

Automation

You set a clear task and the AI carries it out. Example: reformatting a clinic letter into a standard template.

You and AI think together

Augmentation

A back-and-forth partnership. Example: drafting a patient leaflet, then refining tone and reading level together.

AI acts on your behalf

Agency

You set up an AI that works independently and takes actions. Example: an agent that sorts incoming referrals by the rules you gave it.

Take-home

One line for each D

Delegation: decide first what is yours to do, and what is safe to share with AI.
Description: give context, purpose, and format, not just a topic.
Discernment: check the result, and check how the AI got there.
Diligence: you remain accountable, so be open about AI use and protect patients.
